The qchain is the 13th Chamber chain: a small, self-contained ledger with its own blocks,
transactions, treasury and assets. QSAM is the transferable asset on it.

## What it does today

- Blocks, signed transactions, a mempool and a verifiable chain walk.
- A treasury that funds the airdrop from chain supply, not from another network.
- A public read API and one write path, `/api/tx`.
- A Kotlin verifier in the node repository, alongside the Python node.

## What it does not do yet

Post-quantum signing is wired in code and on the roadmap for general use, not finished for every
wallet path. The docs say so plainly rather than implying it is done. When a claim cannot be
measured, it is not published as a result.

## Wake-ups

The public node runs on a free tier and sleeps when it is idle. The first call after a quiet
spell can take up to a minute to answer. That is a cold start, not an outage, and the hourly
monitor is tuned to wait it out rather than page anyone.
