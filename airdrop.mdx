description: "One thousand QSAM per registered wallet, sent from the chain treasury."
---

Every registered wallet receives 1,000 QSAM from the treasury as a signed transaction on our own chain. It is not a transfer on Solana, and it does not depend on another network's state.

## Terms, in plain language

- One allocation per wallet, and the treasury log is public.
- The airdrop page lives at [13thchamberllc.com/1000-qsam-airdrop](https://13thchamberllc.com/1000-qsam-airdrop/).
- Nothing is sold. There is no price and no payment step anywhere in the flow.

## Checking an allocation

Query the address and look for the treasury transaction:

```bash
curl -s https://qsam-qchain.onrender.com/api/address/<your-address>
```

`transactions` lists what the chain has recorded, and `next_nonce` tells you the next value to sign with.
