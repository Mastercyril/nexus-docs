title: "Models"
description: "What leads, what is called, and when a backup takes over."
---

- **Lead:** QAI2, the 13th Chamber foundation model.
- **Deep mode:** Argon and multi-model synthesis, used when a job genuinely needs it.
- **Images:** FLUX.1 schnell, then FLUX.2 klein, then seedream lite. Gemini image and a free renderer are the fallbacks.
- **Voice:** Puter TTS with OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Gemini and neural engines.
- **Video:** Puter video models, billed to your own account.

Rules: the foundation model leads, external engines are called only for the job they are good at, and a backup engages only after the lead has genuinely failed twice.
